Cockpit Lights and Reasons

This document outlines various cockpit light indications and their corresponding reasons, categorized for clarity.

---

Flight Deck Warnings & Indications

#1 AC GEN CAUTION
Supply is not delivered to the bus.
#1 AC GEN HOT
AC generator temperature is more than 166°C.
#1 BLEED HOT
Sensor senses high temperature of the duct line (overpressure, duct leak) in ECS, de-ice system, door seal pressurization, or aircraft pressurization.
#1 DC GEN
DC generator system may be inoperative; supply is not delivered.
#1 DC GEN HOT
DC generator temperature is more than 166°C.
#1 ENG FADEC FAIL
A problem in the engine FADEC, which cannot control the engine.
#1 ENG FUEL PRESS
Engine fuel pressure is low.
#1 ENG HYD PUMP
Engine Driven Pump (EDP) pressure is less than 1800 psi.
#1 ENG OIL PRESS
Engine oil pressure is low.
#1 ENGINE FADEC
Any problem with the FADEC.
#1 FUEL FILTER BYPASS
Indicates a signal from the fuel filter impending switch.
#1 HYD FLUID HOT
Indication of high hydraulic fluid temperature.
#1 HYD ISO VALVE
Isolation valve is closed, isolating a part of the system.
#1 PEC
Propeller Electronic Unit (PECU) senses a fault.
#1 RUD HYD MAINTENANCE
Maintenance action required. Hydraulic pressure is less than 350 psi, and/or CH 1 of the FCECU's LVDT is not moving.
#1 STALL SYS FAIL
Problem in the stall system.
#1 STBY HYD PUMP HOT
SPU (Standby Pump Unit) is operating at high temperature.
#1 TANK FUEL LOW
Left fuel tank collector has less than 139 kg of fuel.
#2 AC GEN
Temperature is more than 166°C.
#2 AC GEN HOT
Sensor senses high temperature of the duct line.
#2 BLEED HOT
Supply is not delivered.
#2 DC GEN
Temperature is more than 166°C.
#2 DC GEN HOT
Problem in the engine FADEC, which cannot control the engine.
#2 ENG FADEC
Any problem with the FADEC.
#2 ENG FADEC FAIL
EDP pressure is less than 1800 psi.
#2 ENG FUEL PRESS
Engine oil pressure is low.
#2 ENG HYD PUMP
Any problem with the FADEC.
#2 ENG OIL PRESS
Receives a signal from the fuel filter impending switch.
#2 FUEL FLTR BYPASS
Fuel filter impending switch has been triggered.
#2 HYD FLUID HOT
Indication of high hydraulic fluid temperature.
#2 HYD ISO VALVE
Isolation valve is closed, isolating a part of the system.
#2 PEC
Propeller Electronic Unit senses a fault.
#2 RUD HYD MAINTENANCE
Hydraulic pressure is less than 350 psi.
#2 STALL SYS FAIL
Problem in the stall system.
#2 STBY HYD PUMP HOT
SPU is operating at high temperature.
#2 TANK FUEL LOW
Right fuel tank collector has less than 139 kg of fuel.
#3 STBY HYD PUMP MAINTENANCE
System pressure is less than 900 psi, or the DCMP has operated for more than 60 seconds on the ground.
AUX BATT HOT
Auxiliary battery is not connected to the left main feeder bus for charging.
AUXILIARY BATTERY
Auxiliary battery-related issue.
CABIN DUCT HOT
Cabin compartment duct temperature is more than 88°C.
CABIN PACK HOT
Cabin pack discharge temperature is more than 212°C.
CABIN PRESS
Cabin pressure is more than 10,000 ft.
DC BUS
#1 DC GCU or #2 DC GCU has sensed an overcurrent for more than 7 seconds.
DEICE PRESS
Pressure is not sensed by the TMU; de-ice manifold pressure is less than 12.5 psig as sensed by the low-pressure switch.
ELEVATOR ASYMMETRY
Difference between two elevators is more than 4.5° (above 185 knots) or 6° (less than 185 knots).
ELEVATOR FEEL
FCECU loses control over the feel actuator, fails to excite the actuator LVDT, or sensed signals are not equal.
ELEVATOR PRESS MAINTENANCE
Maintenance action required; all three systems have high pressure.
EMER LTS DISARMED
Power supply is not armed.
ENG ADP HEAT 1
TMU senses that the main adapter heater and nack heater have failed.
ENG ADPT HEAT 2
TMU senses that the main adapter heater and nack heater have failed.
FLAP DRIVE
FCU senses hydraulic pressure with no flap selection, difference between flap position selection and indication, or FCU malfunction.
FLAP POWER
Flap asymmetry, uncommanded movement, or malfunction of position/lever transducers, arming switch, or FCU.
FLT COMP DUCT HOT
Flight compartment duct temperature is more than 88°C.
FLT COMP PACK HOT
Flight compartment pack discharge temperature is more than 212°C.
FLT DATA RECORDER
System may be inoperative; CVR must be operative, and FDR is not operating.
FUELLING ON
Refuel/defuel panel is open.
FUSELAGE DOORS WARNING
System may be inoperative; any fuselage door proximity sensor is not pressed.
GPWS
GPWC is supplying invalid data to the GPWSC.
ICE DETECT FAIL
Both ice detector probes have failed.
INBO ANTISKID
System is unable to command and control the inboard antiskid system.
INTERNAL BAGG DOOR
Switch target is not pressed.
L AC BUS
Supply is not delivered to the bus due to an overcurrent condition for more than 7 seconds.
L TRU
TRU is not generating supply.
LDG GEAR INOP
PSEU (Proximity Sensor Electronic Unit) failure.
MAIN BATT HOT
Battery temperature is more than 71°C; on the electrical system page, the main battery temp turns red.
MAIN BATTERY
Battery is not getting charged. EPCU shows fault 33, or K8 drive failure.
NOSE STEERING INOP
Any problem in the nose steering system.
OUTBO ANTISKID
Loss of ability to control the outboard spoiler system.
PARKING BRAKE MAINTENANCE
Maintenance action required; emergency brake is set.
PITCH TRIM
Either channel has lost control of the pitch trim actuator.
PITOT HEAT 1
Probe heater has failed.
PITOT HEAT 2
Probe heater has failed.
PITOT HEAT STBY
Standby probe heater has failed.
PROP DEICE
Any reason related to the propeller deicing system.
PUSHER SYST FAIL
Any problem in the pusher system.
R AC BUS
Supply is not delivered to the bus.
R TRU
TRU is not generating supply.
ROLL SPLR INBO
Hydraulic pressure is less than 900 psi and airspeed is less than 165 knots.
ROLL SPLR OUTBO
Ground lift dump valve is not operating correctly.
ROLL SPLR PUTBO
Hydraulic pressure is less than 900 psi and airspeed is less than 165 knots.
ROLL SPOILER INBO
Ground lift dump valve is not operating correctly.
RUD CTRL
Both channels cannot control pressure to the PCU; both system pressures are less than 350 psi, or airspeed is invalid.
SIDE WDO HOT
Side window temperature is more than 60°C.
SMOKE
Smoke is sensed in any baggage compartment.
SPLR OUTBO
FCECU does not prevent spoiler operation when airspeed is more than 185 knots, or FCECU prevents spoiler operation when speed is less than 186 knots.
STBY BATT HOT
Battery temperature is more than 71°C; on the electrical system page, the standby battery temp turns red.
STBY BATTERY
Battery is not getting charged. EPCU shows fault 29, or K25 drive failure.
TOUCHED RUNWAY
Sensor in the aft of the fuselage has been pressed.
WSHLD CTRL
Any problem with the windshield control heating.
WSHLD HOT
Windshield temperature is more than 60°C.
WT ON WHEEL MAINTENANCE
Maintenance action required; WOW1 and WOW2 calculations are different, one proximity sensor in each channel has malfunctioned, or nose wheel overtravel is more than 120°.
