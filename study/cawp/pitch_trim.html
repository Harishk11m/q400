Fault Isolation Procedure: PITCH TRIM Caution Light

This procedure is for isolating a fault when the Caution And Warning Panel (CAWP) PITCH TRIM caution light is on.

The CAWP PITCH TRIM caution light illuminates when the Flight Control Electronic Control Unit (FCECU) detects one of the following conditions:

  • Internal failure of the FCECU
  • Left pitch trim actuator malfunction
  • Right pitch trim actuator malfunction
  • ADC1 or ADC2 malfunction or mismatch
  • Pilot and co-pilot commands are different.

Additionally, the following caution lights may also illuminate:

  • CAWP ELEVATOR FEEL
  • CAWP RUD CTRL
  • CAWP SPLR OUTBD
